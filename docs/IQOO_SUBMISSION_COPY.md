# TITLE
Kaaragir: AI-Powered Artisan Commerce

# ONE-LINE
An AI-powered hyperlocal commerce platform that connects customers with local artisans, turns voice, text and reference images into customizable products, and builds trust through material traceability and milestone-based commerce.

# SHORT DESCRIPTION
Local artisans possess incredible skill but struggle to digitize, while customers find it tedious to communicate custom furniture specifications online. Kaaragir solves this by embedding a multimodal AI Copilot directly on your phone.

Leveraging Whisper and Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct running entirely on-device, customers can simply speak their requirements and attach a reference sketch. The AI instantly extracts dimensions, materials, and stylistic intent into a structured specification, bypassing clumsy dropdown menus. Using the iQOO Office Kit, this specification flows seamlessly to the user's desktop, rendering a live 3D procedural model and calculating volumetric pricing instantly.

To combat material fraud, Kaaragir firewalls the AI from payments. Instead, the inferred material requirement is allocated against a secure Material Registry. Customers verify actual supplier invoices via the Material Passport, and funds are disbursed to artisans only through a strict, transparent Escrow Milestone tracker. 

Kaaragir is not just a chatbot—it is an end-to-end trusted commerce engine that democratizes access to bespoke, heirloom-quality craftsmanship.
