# Kaaragir Platform Overview

## Tagline
*Where Heritage Craftsmanship Meets AI-Powered Hyperlocal Commerce.*

## Overview
Kaaragir bridges the gap between master local artisans and discerning buyers. Through on-device multimodal AI, customers speak natural requirements, attach sketches, and preview live 3D models with deterministic volumetric pricing. All transactions are protected by a digital Escrow vault and backed by verified Material Passports.
